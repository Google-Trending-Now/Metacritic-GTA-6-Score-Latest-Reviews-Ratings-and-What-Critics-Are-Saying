What Does a 99 Metascore Actually Mean?

Metacritic uses a weighted scoring system to calculate its Metascore. A score of 99 is exceptionally high and indicates overwhelmingly positive professional reviews.

  • 90–100: Universal Acclaim
  • 75–89: Generally Favorable Reviews
  • 50–74: Mixed or Average Reviews
  • 20–49: Generally Unfavorable Reviews
  • 0–19: Overwhelming Dislike

A 99 Metascore puts GTA 6 in the highest rating category. However, a high critic score does not mean every player will have exactly the same experience.
